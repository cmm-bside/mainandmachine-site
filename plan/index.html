What exactly will I receive?

A concise written recommendation for the workflow you describe: what to fix first, a practical first step, where automation may help, and what still needs checking. See a complete sample.

Is this really free? Do I have to book a call?

It’s free, with no call or purchase required. We’ll email the plan within 24 hours of your request. You can use the recommendation yourself, share it with your team, or ask us to help.

How is this different from the paid audit?

The free plan is an initial recommendation on one workflow, based on what you tell us. We haven’t inspected your systems or validated savings. The paid AI Readiness Audit investigates your operation, prioritizes opportunities, and develops an ROI model and build plan.

Will you recommend AI for everything?

No. The best first move may be a clearer process, an existing tool, or conventional automation. We’ll explain the recommendation and flag assumptions. Your team keeps control of business decisions.
