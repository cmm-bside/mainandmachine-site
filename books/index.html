Before you choose

A few useful answers.

Which book should I read first?

If you’re deciding how to introduce AI into your company, start with The AI-Ready Owner. If your question is about identity, meaning, and human value in an age of AI, start with What the Machine Cannot Do. You don’t need to read one to understand the other.

Where can I buy the books?

Both books are available on Amazon in Kindle and paperback editions. What the Machine Cannot Do also has a hardcover edition. Use either book’s “Buy on Amazon” button to check current prices and availability in your region.

Do I need to buy a book to get a workflow plan?

No. The free workflow plan is available whether or not you buy either book. Tell us about one repetitive task, and we’ll help you identify a first move. No call is required.

Can Main & Machine help with implementation?

Yes. We scope, build, test, and hand over AI and automation workflows for small and mid-size businesses. Start with a free workflow plan, or explore our services to see how an engagement works.
