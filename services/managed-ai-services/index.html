What the retainer does

Keep the working system useful.

Watch the workflow

Tests and monitoring help identify when a deployed system stops behaving as expected. Your team can flag misses so those examples inform tuning and review.

Maintain the dependencies

Models, APIs and connected tools change. Maintenance addresses the changes relevant to the systems in your agreed coverage, with testing around the updates.

Improve what is already running

An agreed block of build time supports new automations and improvements. A plain-language monthly report explains what ran, what was caught and what changed.

Decide who will maintain it

Buy support when it solves a real ownership problem.

Choose support when your team needs a maintenance partner

Someone needs to own failed runs, tool changes, access and updates. The retainer helps a small team carry that work without leaving every issue with the person whose day job is somewhere else.

Keep a person accountable inside the business

Your workflow owner remains part of the decision process. They can review the report, flag problems and decide which improvements are worth doing next.

Run it yourself when that is the better fit

Managed Services is optional. If you have the people and capacity to operate the system, you can do that. The implementation handoff still includes training and ownership.

Agree the boundaries in writing
  • The systems covered
  • The monitoring and maintenance scope
  • The improvement time included
  • How your team reports and reviews issues

Before you decide.

01Is Managed Services required?

No. It is optional. You own what we build and your team is trained to operate it, whether you choose ongoing support or manage it yourself.

02Do you maintain systems another firm built?

Our Managed Services offering covers systems we built and understand. Bring an existing system to the assessment if you need help deciding what should happen next.

03What does annual payment change?

Pay annually and the retainer runs 12 months for the price of 10; unused months are refunded if you leave.

04Does the retainer cover unlimited new work?

The retainer includes an agreed block of improvement work. The written scope defines the systems covered and the build time included; a new workflow or larger change needs its scope agreed.
