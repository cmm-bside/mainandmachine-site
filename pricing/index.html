Fair questions

How much does AI implementation cost?

01How much does AI implementation cost for a small business?+

The AI Readiness Audit runs $3,500–$8,500 and takes 2 to 4 weeks. An AI Implementation Sprint runs $18,000–$60,000, quoted fixed in writing before work begins. Managed Services runs from $1,500 a month with no lock-in, and a Full Back Office build starts at $95,000. For how our numbers compare to the wider market — hourly, boutique, Big Four — see What an AI Consultant Costs in 2026; for the monthly costs after the build, see What AI Automation Costs to Run.

02Is there a guarantee?+

Yes, on delivery: if a scoped workflow is not live in your operation within 90 days, we keep building at no charge until it is. The scope is fixed in writing before work begins, so the guarantee is against a spec both sides can read. We do not guarantee ROI numbers — nobody honestly can — but we do guarantee the thing you paid for shows up and runs.

03Does the audit fee count toward a build?+

Yes. 100% of your audit fee credits toward an Implementation Sprint signed within 60 days of the audit, up to 25% of the sprint price. If the audit leads to a build, the diagnosis effectively costs you nothing.

04What does the AI Readiness Audit include?+

A workflow map of your real operations, the handful of places AI actually pays, a phased plan you own outright, and a straight read on whether to build at all. It takes 2 to 4 weeks.

05Why fixed prices instead of hourly?+

Because that is how you would want to be treated. The exact price is quoted in writing before work begins and does not move after we start. You will not sit through three discovery calls to learn what something costs. If you are comparing firms, the ten questions in How to Choose an AI Consultant include this one — and a scope a vendor can’t inflate keeps the number fixed after you choose.

06What happens after the 90 days?+

Your team is trained to run what we built, and you own all of it. If you want us to keep watching, Managed Services covers monitoring and maintenance as models and vendors shift: from $1,500 a month, cancel any month it stops earning its keep, and pay annually for 12 months at the price of 10. Whether to keep a retainer, name an internal owner, or hire is worked through in AI Consultant vs. In-House Hire.
