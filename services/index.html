Questions, answered

Before you decide.

Compare the published prices and bring your specific workflow to the call.

01Is the free assessment a paid audit?

No. The free 30-minute assessment helps establish fit and the next step. The paid audit is a separate 2 to 4 weeks engagement that produces a workflow map and phased plan.

02Can we skip the paid audit?

Yes, when your workflow is already defined and can be scoped. Start with the same free assessment and ask to scope an Implementation Sprint directly.

03What is the audit credit?

100% of your audit fee credits toward a sprint signed within 60 days, up to 25% of the sprint price.

04Do we have to buy ongoing support?

No. Managed Services is optional. You own what we build, and your team is trained to run it.
