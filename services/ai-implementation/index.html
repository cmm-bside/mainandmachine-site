Picture the work

The workflow determines the system.

An inquiry becomes prepared work

A lead response workflow collects the information your team needs, routes it and prepares the next step. Any automatic replies or actions need explicit boundaries agreed for that build.

Records move between the right tools

A system connector reduces manual re-entry between the tools you already use. The scope identifies the source of truth, the data that moves, and how exceptions are handled.

Company knowledge becomes usable

A knowledge base helps people find answers in approved business material. The useful output includes sources a person can check, not simply a fluent answer.

Investment and responsibility

Fixed scope. A written price.

An AI Implementation Sprint is $18,000–$60,000, quoted fixed before work begins. Typical delivery is 4 to 12 weeks, about 90 days per workflow.

If a scoped workflow is not live in your operation within 90 days, we keep building at no charge until it is.

Your workflow owner helps explain the process, make decisions and review the system in operation. Discuss tool access, data handling, third-party costs and what the agreed scope covers before starting.

If discovery is needed first, the AI Readiness Audit creates the plan. 100% of your audit fee credits toward a sprint signed within 60 days, up to 25% of the sprint price.

Only when the opportunity needs more investigation. If your workflow is defined and can be scoped, you can go directly from the free assessment to a written sprint quote.
