Check the fit

Useful when the problem needs a closer look.

You know the work is slow, but the first project is unclear

The audit is for established teams with repeated operational work: intake, documents, follow-up, reporting, or information moving between tools. We work with businesses of 5–100 people and $1M–$50M in revenue.

Bring the people who know the workflow

The useful starting material is the process you run today, examples of the work, the tools involved, and the people responsible for it. Discuss any access or data restrictions in the free assessment before sharing sensitive records.

Already know what to build?

A paid audit is not a mandatory toll. If your workflow is defined, we can discuss direct Implementation Sprint scoping. If a basic tool or process fix would solve it, a custom build may be unnecessary.

Questions, answered

Before you decide.

Compare the published prices and bring your specific workflow to the call.

01Is this the same as the free assessment?

No. The free assessment is a 30-minute conversation about fit and possible next steps. The paid audit takes 2 to 4 weeks and produces a workflow map, ranked opportunities and a phased plan for your business.

02Do I have to hire you to build the plan?

No. You own the audit and can use it with us, another firm, or your own team. The recommendation may be to wait or to use a simpler tool.

03How does the audit credit work?

100% of your audit fee credits toward a sprint signed within 60 days, up to 25% of the sprint price. The cap depends on the sprint price; the written quote makes the credit clear.

04Can we go straight to implementation?

Yes, when you already have a defined workflow and enough detail to scope it. Start with the free assessment, tell us what you want built, and we can quote a sprint without a paid audit.
